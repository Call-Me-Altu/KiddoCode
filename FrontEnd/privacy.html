YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy, in plain language.

Updated 12 September 2026

What we collect

When an adult requests a demo, we collect their name, email and phone number, the learner’s age group, course interest, preferred date, time and timezone, and the consent provided. We do not ask for a child’s full name or date of birth in the demo form.

Why we use it

We use these details to respond to the request, arrange a demo, recommend a suitable course and manage follow-up. Submitting a demo request does not subscribe you to unrelated marketing.

Accounts and access

Optional accounts are intended for adults and parents/guardians. Authentication is handled by Supabase. We do not store your password in our booking database. After verifying your email, your account can display demo requests made with that email address. Only authorised administrators can manage all leads.

Service providers

The website uses Netlify for hosting, Render for the API and notification worker, Supabase for database and authentication, and Resend for demo emails. If WhatsApp notifications are enabled, Meta processes those messages. These providers may process information outside your country.

WhatsApp

WhatsApp links open an external service only when you click them. You choose whether to send the pre-filled message. Internal automated lead alerts, when configured, contain a booking reference rather than the full booking details.

Browser storage and technical information

The site uses session storage for account sessions and anonymous booking retry identifiers. It does not include advertising trackers. Hosting and security providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to operate and protect the service.

Children and parental involvement

A parent or guardian should submit requests for children and manage their accounts. Please do not submit unnecessary sensitive information. Contact us if you believe information about a child was submitted without appropriate involvement.

Retention, corrections and deletion

We retain information for handling enquiries and service administration. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion, or to stop follow-up. Some information may need to remain for applicable obligations or in provider backups for a limited period.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email altamashraeen3@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before providing or deleting account information.
