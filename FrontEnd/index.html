Does my child need coding experience?+

No. We can start with the basics. The demo helps us understand their comfort with computers and choose a suitable course.

What do we need for the demo?+

A laptop or desktop, an internet connection and a little curiosity. We will share the meeting details once the time is confirmed.

Is the demo really free?+

Yes. There is no payment or course purchase required to request the demo.

Do I need to create an account?+

No. Book directly using the form below. An optional adult or parent account lets you view requests made with the same verified email.

Are classes and prices fixed?+

We discuss the learning plan, mentor availability, schedule and fees with you before you decide to enrol.

Is my selected time guaranteed?+

It is a preferred time, not a reserved appointment. Our team will contact you to confirm availability, including any timezone or daylight-saving questions.
